# Talk Like TED

In Carmine Gallo's book "Talk Like TED: The 9 Public-Speaking Secrets of the World's Top Minds," he distills the common elements of successful TED talks into three main principles:

* Emotional: These talks touch the heart and create a connection with the audience. This involves demonstrating passion, mastering the art of storytelling, and having a conversational delivery style.
* Novel: These talks teach the audience something new, providing fresh insights or a different perspective. This could involve "jaw-dropping moments" or surprising information.
* Memorable: These talks are presented in ways that the audience won't forget. This includes elements like humor, sticking to the 18-minute rule, creating vivid mental pictures, and being authentic.
While the book outlines nine specific secrets, they all fall under these three overarching categories of being emotional, novel, and memorable.
